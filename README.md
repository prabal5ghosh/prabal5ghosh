# 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Data Scientist and AI researcher** with interests spanning **medical imaging, multimodal machine learning, deep learning, explainable artificial intelligence, longitudinal disease modelling, and computational healthcare**.

My recent research experience at **INRIA Sophia Antipolis** has focused on medical imaging and artificial intelligence, including:

* 🩻 Multimodal MRI analysis for **Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophy (FSHD)**
* 🧠 Longitudinal disease-progression modelling
* 🔬 Clinical + imaging biomarker integration
* 🤖 Deep learning and representation learning
* 🔎 Explainable AI and concept detection in convolutional neural networks
* 📈 Statistical and machine-learning analysis of biomedical data

Before moving deeper into AI research, I worked at **Freshworks** as a software engineer, where I gained professional experience with **Python, Django, Ruby, Ruby on Rails, backend systems, databases, and full-stack development**.

I completed my **MSc in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Université Côte d'Azur, France**, and previously completed an **MCA at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham**, where I was awarded the **University Gold Medal**.

# 🚀 Current Research Focus

My current interests center around learning disease trajectories from **longitudinal multimodal biomedical data**.

```text
Clinical Data ──────────────┐
                            │
MRI Biomarkers ─────────────┼────► Multimodal Representation
                            │                │
Functional Measures ────────┘                ▼
                                      Latent Disease State
                                              │
                                              ▼
                                Longitudinal / ODE Modeling
                                              │
                                              ▼
                                  Disease Progression
                                      Prediction
```

### Topics I am currently exploring

* Multimodal clinical + MRI modelling
* Longitudinal disease progression
* Latent dynamical systems
* Neural Ordinary Differential Equations
* Variational Autoencoders
* Missing-data modelling
* Mask-aware representation learning
* Generative imputation
* MRI-derived biomarkers
* Muscle fat infiltration analysis
* Personalized disease trajectories
